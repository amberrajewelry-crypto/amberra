Отвечать на запросы категорий: Fashion & Beauty, Lifestyle, Gifts, Home.

**Готовый bio для профиля эксперта:**
> Founder of AMBERRA, an independent jewelry studio working exclusively in natural Baltic amber
> and 925 sterling silver. I authenticate amber, advise on distinguishing genuine succinite from
> copal and glass, and on caring for organic gemstones set in silver.

**Шаблон ответа (amber authentication):**
> There are four fast tests for real Baltic amber. Genuine amber floats in heavily salted water
> (about two tablespoons of salt per cup), where glass and most plastics sink. It is warm to the
> touch within seconds — glass stays cold. Rubbed on wool it builds a static charge strong enough
> to lift a scrap of paper. And under UV light it fluoresces a soft blue. If a "cherry amber" piece
> is perfectly uniform in color with no internal variation, treat it as suspect — natural amber
> always varies. [подпись: Vladimir, Founder, AMBERRA — https://www.amberrajewelry.com]

**Шаблон ответа (silver + amber care):**
> Never put amber jewelry in an ultrasonic cleaner or a silver-dip solution: amber is soft organic
> resin and the vibration or chemicals will dull or crack it. Wipe the silver with a dry polishing
> cloth, keep the piece away from perfume and heat, put it on last after lotion, and store it in a
> soft pouch away from harder stones. Worn regularly, sterling silver actually tarnishes less.
> [подпись + ссылка на H или BLOG]

Ритм: 3–5 ответов в неделю. Один принятый = одна ссылка DR 70+.
