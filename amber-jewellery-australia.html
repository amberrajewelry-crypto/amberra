AMBERRA brings genuine Baltic amber jewellery to Australia — handcrafted rings, earrings, pendants and bracelets set in hallmarked 925 sterling silver, and delivered to every corner of the country, from Sydney and Melbourne to Perth, Brisbane and Adelaide.
