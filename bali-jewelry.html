AMBERRA brings you authentic Bali jewelry made with natural Baltic amber and 925 sterling silver, handcrafted by master silversmiths in Bali. Each piece carries 40 million years of ancient earth and the craft behind every piece.

Bali jewelry is a tradition of handcrafted metalwork rooted in centuries of our artisan culture. The island's silversmiths — concentrated in villages like Celuk, Bali, and Sukawati — are celebrated worldwide for three signature techniques: filigree (delicate twisted wire designs), granulation (tiny metal spheres fused to surfaces), and jawan (applied granulation in traditional patterns). These skills pass from parent to child, making every piece a living piece of cultural heritage.

Frequently Asked Questions — Bali Jewelry

What makes Bali jewelry unique?
Bali jewelry combines ancient silversmithing techniques — filigree, granulation, and jawan — with natural gemstones and spiritual intention. AMBERRA adds natural Baltic amber to these traditions, creating pieces with both aesthetic and historical significance.
Is AMBERRA jewelry designed in Bali?
Yes. Every AMBERRA piece is handcrafted by silversmiths in Bali. Each piece is finished and inspected before shipping.
What materials are used in AMBERRA Bali jewelry?
AMBERRA uses 925 sterling silver and natural Baltic amber — a 40-million-year-old fossilized resin containing up to 8% succinic acid. All silver is hallmarked and rhodium-plated for tarnish resistance.
Can I buy Bali jewelry online from AMBERRA?
Yes. AMBERRA ships worldwide from Bali. All pieces come with free gift wrapping, a certificate of authenticity, and a 30-day return policy. Free worldwide shipping on orders over $200.
