Choosing the Right Amber Color

Amber occurs in a wider color range than most people realize. At AMBERRA, our ring collection features:

  • Cognac amber — deep honey-brown. The most classic and versatile amber color. Suits warm skin tones particularly well.
  • Golden amber — bright yellow-gold. Vivid and joyful. Pairs well with yellow gold settings.
  • Cherry amber — deep red-tinted brown. Dramatic and rich. A statement color.
  • Milky amber — opaque, creamy yellow-white. Soft and unusual. Suits minimalist aesthetics.
  • Green amber — rare. Olive to forest green tones. Striking and uncommon. High collector value.

Ring Width and Finger Shape

Wider band rings suit longer fingers and make a stronger visual statement. Slimmer bands with smaller stones are more flattering on shorter fingers and create a more delicate look. Our product descriptions include band width in millimeters to help you choose.

Which Ring Settings Suit Which Lifestyles?

For everyday wear, we recommend bezel settings — the amber is fully enclosed by the silver, protecting the stone from impact. For occasional wear, prong or open settings showcase more of the amber's color and translucency. If you work with your hands, choose a low-profile setting where the stone sits flush with or just above the band.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular amber ring color for women?
Cognac amber is consistently our best-selling color for rings — the deep honey-brown tone is universally flattering and versatile with most wardrobe colors. Cherry amber is popular as a statement piece.
How do I find my ring size for amber rings?
Measure the circumference of your finger in millimeters using a strip of paper, then refer to our size conversion chart (US / EU / UK). Alternatively, use the free ring sizer tool at checkout.
Are amber rings suitable for everyday wear?
Yes. AMBERRA amber rings in bezel settings are suitable for daily wear. Avoid wearing rings during heavy manual work, gardening, or contact sports. Remove before swimming to prevent chlorine contact with the silver.
What makes an amber ring a good investment?
Natural amber, especially high-quality Balinese varieties with unusual colors or clear inclusions, has appreciated in value over decades. Handcrafted sterling silver settings add additional value. An AMBERRA piece is a better investment than mass-produced jewelry.
