What Makes Amber Jewelry "Authentic"?

Genuine amber is fossilized tree resin at least one million years old. Baltic amber is typically 40–50 million years old, formed from the resin of ancient forests in the Baltic region. When buying amber jewelry online, look for:

  • Certificate of authenticity — a written statement confirming natural amber origin
  • Transparent sourcing — the brand should be able to tell you where the amber comes from
  • No claims of "enhanced" or "reconstructed" amber — these terms often mean pressed amber powder or synthetic resin
  • Visible natural inclusions — genuine amber often contains air bubbles, plant matter, or flow lines visible under magnification

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if amber jewelry is real?
Request a certificate of authenticity from the seller. Genuine amber floats in a saturated salt water solution (1 cup salt per 2 cups water), while plastic sinks. Real amber also becomes electrically charged when rubbed and may attract small paper pieces.
