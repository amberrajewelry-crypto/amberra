Weight and Comfort

A common concern with drop and dangle earrings is weight. Natural amber is one of the lightest gemstones available — far lighter than glass, crystal, or most semi-precious stones. An AMBERRA amber drop earring typically weighs between 2–4 grams. Our clients consistently report that they forget they are wearing them.

Matching Amber Earrings with Other Jewelry

Amber earrings pair naturally with amber pendants, amber bracelets, and gold or silver chains. The warm tones of cognac and golden amber work particularly well with yellow gold settings. Cherry and green amber create striking contrast when paired with white gold or rhodium-plated silver.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular amber earring style?
At AMBERRA, amber drop earrings and stud earrings are the best-sellers. Drop earrings suit most face shapes and work for both casual and formal occasions.
Are amber earrings suitable for sensitive ears?
Yes. All AMBERRA earrings use 925 sterling silver posts and findings, which are hypoallergenic. The amber itself is a natural organic material with no known skin irritants.
How much do amber earrings cost?
AMBERRA amber earrings start from $49 for studs and range up to $149 for large statement chandelier designs. Free worldwide shipping on orders over $200.
Do amber earrings chip or scratch easily?
Amber has a hardness of 2–2.5 on the Mohs scale, softer than most gemstones. Avoid wearing amber earrings in situations with impact risk. Store separately from harder jewelry to prevent surface scratching.
