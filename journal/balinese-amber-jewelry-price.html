What Determines the Price of Amber Jewelry?

Stone size and rarity: Larger amber stones command higher prices. Rare colors — particularly green amber, blue amber (extremely rare in Balinese varieties), and clear golden amber with visible inclusions — carry premium pricing.

Setting complexity: A simple bezel setting requires less labor than a multi-element sculptural setting. Pieces with intricate filigree or granulation in the silver work reflect higher craft hours and therefore higher prices.

Silver content: All AMBERRA pieces use 925 sterling silver. This is a meaningful cost factor — we do not use silver-plated base metal or vermeil.

Is Expensive Amber Better?

Not necessarily. A $59 amber ring from AMBERRA is made by the same hands, to the same standard, with the same certifications as a $215 piece. The price difference reflects stone size, color rarity, and setting complexity — not quality tier. Our entry-level pieces are not "budget" items; they are simply smaller or simpler designs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much should I pay for a genuine amber ring?
Genuine handcrafted amber rings in sterling silver 925 typically start around $50–70 for smaller stones and simple settings. Expect $100–200 for mid-size stones with detailed settings. Be cautious of amber rings priced under $20 — they are almost certainly synthetic.
Why is some amber jewelry so expensive?
Price reflects stone rarity (color, size, inclusions), handcraft labor, silver content, and brand positioning. Rare colors like green or cherry amber command premiums. Handcrafted pieces from Bali cost more than factory-produced jewelry — that reflects real artisan time and skill.
Is AMBERRA amber jewelry good value for money?
AMBERRA prices are positioned at the mid-range for handcrafted amber jewelry from Bali — below high-fashion jewelry brands, above fast-fashion jewelers. Every piece is handmade, certified natural amber, and backed by a 30-day return policy.
Do amber prices change?
Amber prices can shift based on supply from Indonesian mining regions and global demand. AMBERRA prices are stable year-round, with occasional seasonal promotions.
