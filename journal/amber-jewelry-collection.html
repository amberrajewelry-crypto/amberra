Collection by Category

Earrings (45 designs) — the largest category in our collection. From $49 stud earrings to $149 chandelier designs. Includes studs, drops, dangles, hoops, and chandeliers in cognac, cherry, golden, milky, and green amber.

Rings (20 designs) — from $59 to $215. Solitaire, cluster, statement, and stackable designs. Available in US sizes 5–9. Cognac and golden amber are the most popular stone colors in our ring collection.

Bracelets (19 designs) — from $69 to $225. Link, beaded, cuff, and charm styles. Sized XS through L (15–19cm). Several designs are suitable for stacking.

Pendants (8 designs) — from $69 to $159. Includes bezel, open-back, sculptural, and raw amber styles. All pendants include a sterling silver chain in your choice of length.

Chains (2 designs) — from $79. Pure sterling silver chains with amber accent beads. Designed to layer with pendants or wear alone.

New Arrivals

AMBERRA adds new designs seasonally. New arrivals are marked on the shop page and featured first in each category. Subscribe to our newsletter to be notified of new pieces before they are announced publicly.

Limited Edition and One-of-a-Kind Pieces

Several pieces in the AMBERRA collection are produced as single units — one stone, one setting, never to be repeated. These are marked "One of a Kind" in the shop. If you find one of these pieces, it is genuinely unrepeatable: the stone was unique, and the piece was made specifically around its dimensions and color.

Custom Orders

AMBERRA accepts custom jewelry commissions. If you are looking for a specific amber color, stone size, or setting style not available in the current collection, contact us with your requirements. Custom pieces are produced in Ubud and delivered within 4–6 weeks.
