About Bali Silver Rings

AMBERRA Bali silver rings are crafted by master silversmiths in the villages of Bali, the island's cultural heart. Each ring begins as raw 925 sterling silver, hand-drawn into wire or sheet, then shaped and soldered using techniques passed down through generations of Balinese artisans.

The amber stones are sourced from Balinese deposits — fossilized tree resin formed over 40 million years. No two pieces of natural amber are identical: variations in color (from pale gold to deep cognac), inclusions, and transparency make each stone a singular object. Our silversmiths assess each stone individually before designing a setting that honors its natural form.

Our ring collection spans 20 designs across three categories: solitaire settings, cluster designs, and band rings with amber inlay. Solitaire rings showcase a single amber cabochon in a bezel or prong setting — these are the most popular choice for everyday wear. Cluster designs combine multiple smaller stones in geometric or organic arrangements. Band rings feature amber inlay within the silver band itself, creating a continuous line of warm color.

Every AMBERRA ring is finished by hand with light oxidation to enhance the detail work, then polished to a mirror finish. Before shipping, each piece undergoes a ritual blessing ceremony performed by a Balinese priest — a practice our founder Jane Sakhvadze considers essential to the brand's identity.

Rings are available in US sizes 5–9 (EU 49–59). All orders include a complimentary ring sizer, care instructions in four languages, and AMBERRA's signature gift packaging: a kraft box with amber ribbon, sealed with our wax stamp. Free worldwide shipping on orders over $200.
