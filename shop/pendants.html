About Amber Pendants from Bali

AMBERRA amber pendant necklaces are designed to be worn close to the skin — the traditional Balinese way of connecting the wearer to the stone's energy. Each pendant is handcrafted in Bali using 925 sterling silver and a single natural amber stone, chosen by our artisans for its color, clarity, and character.

Amber has been used as adornment and talisman across cultures for thousands of years. In Bali, the tradition of wearing amber at the throat — the seat of communication and voice — carries deep symbolic significance. AMBERRA's pendant collection is designed with this tradition in mind: simple settings that allow the stone to be the focus, worn on a chain that positions it naturally at the collarbone or sternum.

Our pendant collection includes eight designs. Teardrop pendants feature an elongated amber cabochon in a wire-wrapped or bezel setting — the most classic form. Round pendants use spherical amber stones in minimal silver settings. Geometric pendants incorporate angular amber shapes: triangles, hexagons, and marquise cuts. Statement pendants combine multiple stones or feature amber in combination with other Balinese materials such as black coral or shell.

Each pendant is sold with a matching 925 sterling silver chain. Chain lengths are available in 40cm, 45cm, 50cm, and 60cm — a full-length guide is included with every order. 40cm sits at the collarbone; 50cm at the sternum; 60cm below the bust.

AMBERRA pendants range from $79 to $245. All include our standard gift packaging, care card, and certificate of amber authenticity. For custom chain lengths or bespoke settings, contact us at hello@amberrajewelry.com.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the amber pendant come with a chain?
Yes. All AMBERRA pendants include a 925 sterling silver chain in your choice of length: 40cm, 45cm, 50cm, or 60cm.
Are amber pendants suitable as gifts?
Absolutely. No sizing required, and the warm tones of Baltic amber suit all skin tones. Arrives in our signature gift box with a handwritten note option.
How do I care for a Baltic amber pendant?
Remove before swimming or applying perfume. Wipe clean with a soft cloth. Store in the pouch provided to prevent scratching from other jewelry.
Can I buy just the pendant without a chain?
Yes. Select 'pendant only' at checkout. The pendant includes a bail fitting compatible with any standard chain.
