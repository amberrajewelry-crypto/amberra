About Bali Bracelets

AMBERRA Bali bracelets are crafted using three traditional Balinese techniques: weaving, casting, and bead-stringing. Each method produces a distinct type of bracelet, and each is performed entirely by hand in our Bali workshop.

Woven silver bracelets use fine-gauge sterling silver wire drawn through a traditional Balinese weaving form — a labor-intensive process that can take up to six hours per piece. Amber stones are set into the weave at regular intervals, appearing as warm points of color within the silver surface. Cast bracelets are made by pouring molten silver into hand-carved wax molds, then finishing the surface by hand with chisels and files. Bead bracelets string natural amber beads on a reinforced silk cord with a silver clasp — these are lighter in weight and more flexible in sizing.

Our bracelet collection spans 19 designs. Adjustable fit bracelets are available in XS (15cm) through L (19cm) with a lobster clasp and 2cm extension chain. Bangle styles are available in small (inner diameter 58mm) and standard (63mm). All include a sizing guide.

Amber used in AMBERRA bracelets comes from Balinese deposits. Colors range from transparent golden honey to deep opaque cognac. Each stone is polished to a smooth cabochon, then inspected for surface quality before setting. No two bracelets are exactly alike — minor variations in stone color, silver patination, and wire texture are natural expressions of handcraft, not defects.

All AMBERRA bracelets include our signature gift box, amber care card, certificate of authenticity, and bracelet sizing guide. Orders over $200 ship free worldwide. Returns and exchanges accepted within 30 days for unworn items.

What sizes are Bali bracelets available in?
AMBERRA Bali bracelets are available in XS (15cm), S (16cm), M (17cm), and L (19cm). Measure your wrist and add 1.5–2cm for a comfortable fit.
Can I stack Bali bracelets?
Yes. Our beaded and link bracelet styles are designed for stacking. The warm amber tones complement metals, leather, and other gemstone bracelets.

Several AMBERRA bracelet designs — particularly our beaded and link styles — suit all genders. Unisex sizing is available.
How are Bali bracelets made?
