About Balinese Earrings

The AMBERRA earrings collection is our largest — over 45 designs ranging from barely-there studs to dramatic statement drops. Each pair is handcrafted, by silversmiths who have spent years mastering the traditional Balinese art of wire-drawing, granulation, and stone-setting.

Baltic amber earrings have been worn as protective talismans for centuries. In traditional Balinese culture, amber was believed to carry the energy of sunlight — warm, sustaining, and clarifying. AMBERRA honors this tradition by using only natural, unprocessed amber stones: no heating, no dyeing, no resin enhancement. What you receive is exactly what the earth produced.

Our earring styles are organized into four families. Drop earrings feature one or more amber stones suspended below a silver post or hook — these range from 2cm to 8cm in length and include both delicate single-stone designs and elaborate multi-tier arrangements. Stud earrings are our most versatile style: a single amber cabochon set flush in a silver bezel, available in cognac, honey, cherry, and green amber. Hoop earrings integrate amber into a continuous silver curve. Chandelier earrings are AMBERRA's signature: wide, layered structures combining multiple amber stones with fine filigree silverwork.

All earrings use surgical-grade 925 sterling silver posts and findings — hypoallergenic, nickel-free, and suitable for sensitive ears. Each pair comes individually wrapped in tissue, placed in AMBERRA's signature gift box with a ribbon, care card, and certificate of amber authenticity.

For international orders, AMBERRA ships worldwide via tracked courier. Standard delivery: 7–14 business days. Express: 3–5 business days. All orders over $200 qualify for free standard shipping.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Balinese earrings hypoallergenic?
Yes. All AMBERRA earrings use 925 sterling silver posts and findings, which are hypoallergenic and nickel-free.
What styles of Balinese earrings are available?
Studs, drop earrings, dangle earrings, hoop earrings, and chandelier designs. Over 45 styles in cognac, cherry, golden, and green amber.
How much do Balinese earrings cost?
AMBERRA Balinese earrings start from $49 for studs and range to $149 for statement chandelier designs.
Do Balinese earrings come gift-wrapped?
Yes. All AMBERRA orders include our signature gift box with ribbon, care card, and certificate of authenticity at no extra charge.
What is the difference between amber colors in Balinese earrings?
Cognac amber is deep warm brown; honey amber is golden yellow; cherry amber is red-orange; green amber is rare and highly valued. All are natural color variations — no dyes or treatments used.
