Wholesale Bali Jewelry — FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for AMBERRA wholesale Bali jewelry?

Our Starter wholesale tier begins at 10 pieces per order with no category restrictions — you can mix rings, earrings, pendants, and bracelets freely. The Partner tier starts at 30 pieces, and our Exclusive program begins at 100 pieces with the best available pricing and reserved stock allocations.

What is the wholesale price of Bali jewelry from AMBERRA?

Wholesale pricing starts at 40–50% below retail (retail prices range from $39–$215). Your exact tier pricing is confirmed in writing after application. No hidden fees — the price quoted includes packaging and Certificate of Authenticity. Shipping is quoted separately based on destination and order weight.

Do you ship wholesale Bali jewelry internationally?

Yes. We ship to 50+ countries from Bali via DHL and FedEx Express. All wholesale shipments include full cargo insurance, tracking, and commercial invoice. Typical transit time is 3–7 business days to Europe and North America. Customs documentation is prepared by our team.

What types of businesses qualify for the wholesale program?

We work with boutiques, concept stores, jewelry retailers, hotel and resort gift shops, wellness spas, and online resellers. We do not require a physical storefront — e-commerce sellers are welcome with proof of platform presence (Etsy, Amazon, own website). We do not sell wholesale to individual buyers.

Is AMBERRA Bali jewelry certified authentic for resale?

Yes. Every AMBERRA piece ships with a Certificate of Authenticity confirming natural Baltic amber (40-million-year-old fossilized resin, not copal or glass) and 925 sterling silver. Wholesale orders include individual certificates for each piece, ready for resale. We also provide product photography and copy for your online store upon request.
